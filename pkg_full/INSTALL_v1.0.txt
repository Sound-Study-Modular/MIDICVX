MIDICVX v1.0 experimental firmware

LIVE (ARP off): original Erica firmware behavior.
ARP + 1 Voice (switch up):
  CV/GATE 2 = live lowest-note bass.
  CV/GATE 1 = arp of every held note except the lowest.
ARP + 2 Voice (switch down):
  All held notes are sorted and split dynamically.
  Lower half (rounded up) -> CV/GATE 2 arp.
  Upper half -> CV/GATE 1 arp.
Yellow gate LEDs follow the physical gate outputs.

Install:
  cp playback.c playback.h ~/Desktop/foundation/
  cd ~/Desktop/foundation
  make clean
  make
