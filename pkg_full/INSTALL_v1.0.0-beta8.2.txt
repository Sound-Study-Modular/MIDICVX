MIDICVX v1.0.0-beta8.2

Replace main.c, main.h, and midi.c.

Build:
  cd ~/Desktop/foundations
  make clean
  make MCU=atmega328p

Flash:
  avrdude -c usbasp -p m328p -B 100 -U flash:w:build/midi_cv_x.hex:i

Startup split learning:
- Both yellow LEDs stay on for 2 seconds after normal runtime begins.
- Play the desired split key while they are on.
- The lower red LED flashes when the Note On is received.
- Both yellow LEDs blink 3 times when the split is saved.
- The chosen key and all higher notes belong to the ARP side.
