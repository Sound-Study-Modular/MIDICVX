MIDICVX v1.0.0-beta6 — ARP 1 Voice routing rewrite

Replace playback.c, then build normally.

ARP 1 Voice fixed routing:
- MIDI notes 0 through 36 -> CV1/GATE1 live bass only
- MIDI notes 37 through 127 -> CV2/GATE2 arpeggiator only

Why note 36:
Many MIDI controllers and DAWs label MIDI note 60 as C3. On those devices,
MIDI note 36 is displayed as C1. The previous note-24 boundary sat below the
playable range and therefore sent nearly everything to CV2.

This revision adds physically separate bass_velocity[] and arp_velocity[] pools.
CV2 cannot read bass notes; CV1 cannot read upper notes in ARP 1 Voice.
