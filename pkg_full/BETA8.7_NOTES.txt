MIDICVX v1.0.0-beta8.7

Stability / transition revision
--------------------------------
- Mode changes silence both outputs, cancel pending retriggers, and reset arp history.
- Held MIDI notes are preserved across mode changes and rebuilt for the destination mode.
- Algorithm changes restart both arp engines from a clean first step.
- UP restarts from lowest; DOWN from highest; PING-PONG ascending; ORDER PLAYED from first held key.
- RANDOM clears previous-note history and restarts from a deterministic nonzero seed.
- 1 VOICE / 2 VOICE switch changes are applied immediately while notes are held.
- LIVE mode held notes are replayed through the new voice-switch routing.
- MONO/DUAL arp switch changes drop stale gates and rebuild arp pools immediately.
- Split learn and EEPROM persistence behavior are unchanged from beta8.6.1.
