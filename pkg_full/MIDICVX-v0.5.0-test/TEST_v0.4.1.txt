MIDICVX v0.4.1 - Held-note manager

This build adds the 128-note held-velocity table while preserving normal live
MIDI-to-CV behavior. The external transport input and its two red LED indicators
remain unchanged. Transport does not advance notes in this build.

Expected test:
1. Normal MIDI notes, CV, gates, pitch bend and Program-button behavior remain normal.
2. External clock into the former Clock Out jack still flashes both red LEDs.
3. No new audible arpeggiator behavior yet.
