# MIDICVX v0.4.2 — First UP Arpeggiator

- Adds LIVE and ARP UP playback modes.
- Short Program-button press toggles LIVE ↔ ARP UP.
- External clock on the former CLOCK OUT jack advances the arp one note per rising edge.
- Held notes are scanned from lowest to highest and wrap at the top.
- Releasing all held notes lowers the generated gates.
- Existing long-press configuration and power-up calibration behaviors remain.
- Raw external clock continues to illuminate the LED beside the clock jack.
- Qualified transport ticks continue to flash the lower red status LED.
