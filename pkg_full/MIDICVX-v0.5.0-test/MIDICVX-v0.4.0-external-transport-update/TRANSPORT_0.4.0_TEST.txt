MIDICVX 0.4.0 — EXTERNAL TRANSPORT INPUT TEST

What changed
------------
- The former CLOCK OUT jack is now a clock/trigger INPUT.
- PC1 / PCINT9 detects rising edges.
- The front-panel LED physically connected to the clock jack should follow
  the raw incoming pulse.
- The lower MIDI/status LED flashes for about 25 ms for every qualified
  rising edge accepted by the transport engine.
- CV, Gate, Program-button, calibration, and normal MIDI note behavior remain
  pass-through/stock.
- MIDI clock no longer drives the clock jack in this test build.

How to test
-----------
1. Patch a clock or trigger signal into the former CLOCK OUT jack.
2. Start slowly, around 1–4 pulses per second.
3. Expected:
   - Clock-jack LED follows each incoming high pulse.
   - Lower MIDI/status LED gives one visible flash per rising edge.
4. Try a Eurorack clock, trigger output, square-wave LFO, or 5 V logic clock.

Input compatibility
-------------------
The existing PCB protection/conditioning around the jack remains in circuit.
Start with 5 V pulses. Eurorack 8–10 V clock/gate signals should be clamped by
that existing circuit. Lower-voltage 3.3 V clocks may work, but must be tested;
do not assume compatibility until the lower status LED confirms detection.

Important
---------
Do not connect two active outputs together. In this firmware the jack is an
input, so do not feed MIDI clock out expectations into it.
