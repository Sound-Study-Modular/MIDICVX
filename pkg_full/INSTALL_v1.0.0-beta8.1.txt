MIDICVX v1.0.0 beta8.1

Replace main.c only when upgrading from beta8.

Startup split learning:
1. Power on the module.
2. Both yellow gate LEDs remain on for two seconds.
3. Press the desired split key during that two-second window.
4. Both yellow LEDs blink three times when the note is detected and saved.
5. The selected key and all higher keys are the arp zone; lower keys are bass.

Algorithm indication while ARP is enabled:
1 blink  = UP
2 blinks = DOWN
3 blinks = PING-PONG
4 blinks = RANDOM
5 blinks = ORDER PLAYED
