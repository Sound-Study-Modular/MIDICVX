MIDICVX v0.8.0 — CV1 LIVE + CV2 ARP

Replace these files in ~/Desktop/foundation:
  main.c
  playback.c
  playback.h
  firmware_info.h

Commands:
  cd ~/Desktop/foundation
  cp main.c main.c.v080-backup
  cp playback.c playback.c.v080-backup
  cp playback.h playback.h.v080-backup
  cp firmware_info.h firmware_info.h.v080-backup

  cp ~/Downloads/MIDICVX-v0.8.0-CV1-live-CV2-arp/main.c .
  cp ~/Downloads/MIDICVX-v0.8.0-CV1-live-CV2-arp/playback.c .
  cp ~/Downloads/MIDICVX-v0.8.0-CV1-live-CV2-arp/playback.h .
  cp ~/Downloads/MIDICVX-v0.8.0-CV1-live-CV2-arp/firmware_info.h .

  make clean
  make

Flash:
  avrdude -c usbasp -p m328p -U flash:w:build/midi_cv_x.hex:i
